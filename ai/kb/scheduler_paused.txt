scheduler_paused: background scheduling is paused
meaning: the always-on scheduler gives background processes no slices at the prompt or at busy points; services such as tickd and inferd run only while the console waits for a job it started
likely causes: an operator ran sched pause (it is a diagnostic switch) and did not run sched resume
action: resume-scheduler - turn background slices back on
risk: low - it restores the default state of the system
reversible: yes - sched pause, or the verification failing, pauses it again
